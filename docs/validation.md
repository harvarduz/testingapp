# Verified release

The particle hero and page-wide motion revisions were validated on 15 October 2026 with Node.js 24, Chromium and the compiled production frontend. The supplied Bloom Material Expressive reference remains the visual foundation: four tonal palettes, light/dark modes, self-hosted Google Sans Flex/Roboto, a collapsible rail, neutral information surfaces, outlined secondary actions, and filled primary actions. Google blue uses #0b57d0 in light mode and #a8c7fa in dark mode.


The preceding UI/UX audit, validated on 14 October 2026, reproduced and corrected these failures in Chromium, including viewports omitted by earlier checks:

- Landing navigation was missing at 801–1000 px. Its disclosure now shares the desktop breakpoint, closes on Escape/outside/focus/resize, scrolls on short screens, and transfers focus to the destination heading.
- Particle scroll stages used to switch after most of the drawing had left the screen. The audit corrected their timing and added **Follow scroll** recovery with accurate hints for pause/reduced motion. The 15 October revision replaces that small scene with the persistent hero described below.
- The knowledge map compressed its text column to roughly 90 px at 820×1180, and its cards overlapped at intermediate widths. Grid tracks and map spacing now keep the text and cards separate; compact scenes avoid pinning content that cannot fit. The displayed connection count matches completed diagram connections.
- Phone search was squeezed between icons; it now uses a full row. Subject shortcuts scroll horizontally, filter values remain readable, and queried text uses theme colours in dark mode. Closed mobile navigation is inert to keyboard focus.
- Next/retry/results in practice could leave focus on the document body and the new prompt above the viewport. These transitions reveal and focus the new prompt; worked explanations and copied links are announced. Notes accurately distinguish pending, failed and completed saves. Fraction percentages distinguish approximation from equality, and algorithm errors focus the affected field.
- Imported courses could be discarded without a warning. Imports now start with unsaved status; added lessons open with title focus; validation is presented and focused near the top. Published lesson protection updates after saving. Account/editor submissions are guarded against duplicate requests, and import failures offer an appropriate action.
- Account-load failures previously retried only the course endpoint. Retry now reloads the failed session independently, and a revision guard prevents a delayed anonymous response from replacing a newly signed-in account.

Home and the app share one account menu. Names and email come from the real session; repeated account and sync banners have been removed. Sign in opens login, Create account opens registration, and each form focuses its first field. Menus support keyboard navigation, dismiss predictably, preserve focus, and fit 320/390/1366 px. Failed saving offers retry; failed logout preserves the account with recovery feedback. Logout protects unsaved creator work and clears private editor state after success. A delayed login response accepts the account without closing a newer dialog. Review-to-account navigation exposed a queued scroll event that dismissed the menu; dismissal now requires an actual page or anchor movement. The avatar no longer inherits the old hover colour.
